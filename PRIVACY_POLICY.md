# Privacy Policy - Retail POS

**Last Updated:** 2026  
**Developed by:** HoBo Custom Software

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how Retail POS ("Software") developed and owned by HoBo Custom Software handles data in Standalone and Multi-tenant deployments.

## 2. Data Ownership
All business data belongs to the business/user who owns the license or subscription. HoBo Custom Software does not claim ownership of customer business data.

## 3. Deployment Types
- **Standalone (Dedicated):** All data is stored on the customer's own server/database. HoBo Custom Software has no access to this data.
- **SaaS Multi-tenant:** Data is stored on HoBo Custom Software managed infrastructure with strict tenant isolation (row-level). Each tenant can only access their own data.

## 4. Information Collected
The Software may store: business information, product catalog, sales transactions, customer/supplier details, inventory data, user accounts with roles/PINs.

## 5. Data Security
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- PIN-based quick authentication for POS terminals
- Tenant isolation in multi-tenant mode
- License key validation for standalone mode

## 6. Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or share business data with third parties. Data is only accessed as necessary for support with explicit customer permission.

## 7. Changes
HoBo Custom Software reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy as needed.

## 8. Contact
For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact HoBo Custom Software.
